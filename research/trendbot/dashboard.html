Strategy lab

        

      
Nightly research on NNFX, Sneaky Pivot (15m) and Fib / FVG / liquidity-grab strategies: rolling walk-forward (252 days in-sample, 6 months out-of-sample), look-ahead detector, crash stress test. A proposal needs an out-of-sample Sharpe above 1.5 and your approval, then 30 days of paper trading before real money.

      

      
Proposals and approved strategies

      

    


    

      
Market scanner

        

      
Today's movers: up 10%+ since 00:00 UTC, volume 5x its 50-day average, a news / social / unlock catalyst, and under 10 million tokens circulating. Watch-only: add a pair to a bot to trade it (the nine rules still apply).

      

    


    

      
Verification agents

        

      
Independent checkers re-derive what the charts and the bot show. A pair failed by the data, exchange, indicator or price agent gets no new entries until it checks out again.

      

    


    

      
Smart money: other traders

        

      
Ranked by win rate (95% lower bound, so a few lucky wins don't count), profit factor, drawdown and style. Following a qualified trader arms a pair when they buy it: the bot takes that pair's next signal that passes all nine rules. Every trader's losses feed Chantisimo's memory. Solana wallets are watch-only.
